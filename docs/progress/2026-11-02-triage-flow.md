# Triage handoff flow — 2 Nov 2026

## Flow
1. Caller describes the problem.
2. Agent collects a few clarifying details.
3. If symptoms look urgent, escalate immediately.
4. Otherwise share general guidance and offer a clinic handoff.

## Notes
- Keep questions short and one at a time for voice.
- Confirm details back to the caller before acting on them.
- Handoff summary should include the original wording, not a paraphrase only.

## Open items
- Decide what minimum info the clinic needs for a useful handoff.
- Make the handoff message translatable per language.
