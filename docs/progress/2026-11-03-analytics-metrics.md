# Call analytics metrics — 3 Nov 2026

## Metrics that matter
- Total calls and answered calls
- Average turn latency
- Handoff rate
- Fallback / low-confidence rate

## Why these
They tell us whether the assistant is useful, not just whether it runs. Latency and handoff rate are the two signals users feel most.
