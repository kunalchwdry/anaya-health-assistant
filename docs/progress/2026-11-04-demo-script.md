# Demo script draft — 4 Nov 2026

## Scenario
A caller describes a fever and asks what to do.

## Beats
1. Agent greets in the caller's language.
2. Caller explains the symptom.
3. Agent asks one clarifying question.
4. Agent gives general guidance and offers a clinic handoff.
5. Agent confirms the handoff summary.

## Tips
- Keep the demo under three minutes.
- Have a fallback recording in case the network is bad.
- Show the analytics dashboard after the call.

## Follow-ups
- Rehearse the Hindi and English versions.
- Check the emergency escalation beat separately.
